yet confirmed** and must not be presented as established.

Two features require explanation before the sample can be called representative:

1. **1523/1526 together are 24.6%** of all records (134 + 90). This is not a
   uniform exam-year sample; it looks like two early cohorts dominate.
2. **No 第3甲 records at all** (34 第1甲 + 877 第2甲). Either the source only
   codes the first two classes, or the selection excluded 第3甲; this must be
   stated.

`xy_count` (records sharing a coordinate) ranges from 1 to 560, median 69. This
is a place-level join, not a unique residential coordinate; do not interpret it
as 911 independent locations.

## 2. Source hypothesis — needs confirmation

The column set (`NameChn`, `IndexYear`, `EntryChn`, `EntryYear`, `EntryRank`,
`AddrName`, `AddrChn`, `X`, `Y`) is structurally consistent with the **CBDB
Ming dengke lu** export described by 刘京臣 (52 科、14,116 名明代进士). External
comparisons often used:

- CBDB 2025-05-20 release: 649,533 persons (dataset-level count).
- 龚延明考证的明代进士总数: 24,595.
- CBDB 明代登科录: 14,116 records (per 刘京臣).

If the hypothesis is correct, 911 records are about **6.5% of 14,116** and
**3.7% of 24,595**. These are comparisons, not proof of source. **Do not cite
them as the sample's provenance until the author confirms the source and
version.**
